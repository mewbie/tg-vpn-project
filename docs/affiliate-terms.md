Avoid Binance at first — good tier gated behind 5k followers.

## The six traps — read every contract for these
1. **Cookie window.** 30d means no commission if they don't convert in a month.
   PureVPN's 90d is 3x standard and the reason it ranks first.
2. **Last-click attribution.** Another affiliate's link can override yours.
3. **Revshare caps / tier cliffs.** Your rate can drop unilaterally.
4. **Dormant-account rules.** Crypto especially: revshare stops if the user stops trading.
5. **Termination clauses.** They can cut you.
6. **Payout minimums.** Typically $100 — matters at our scale.

## The only real defence
The bridge page. No program gives you the customer; capture the email yourself.
