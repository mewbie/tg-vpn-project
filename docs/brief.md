# Brief — the reasoning

Written so an agent can judge edge cases, not just follow orders.

## Why revshare, not CPA
A flat CPA pays once. Lifetime revshare on a VPN subscription pays monthly for
years. With €250/month we cannot outspend anyone, so we must compound instead of
scale. A CPA operator and a revshare operator running identical traffic are in
structurally different businesses. **Rule: revshare.**

## Why the bridge page
No affiliate program gives you the customer. The user sits on the VPN's rails.
So we insert our own capture point between the ad and the offer. Cost: some
conversion (expect 20–40% drop on the extra step). Benefit: we own the email, so
when the program cuts our rate or dies, we still have the people.
**Rule: never link straight to the offer.**

## Why one axis at a time
Expansion has two axes: audience and product. Change both and a failure is
unattributable. New-audience-same-product is cheapest (reuse program, funnel,
emails). Same-audience-new-product is more valuable (ticket rises, trust exists).
Both good; together bad. **Rule: one axis.**

## Why this niche
Crypto users in restricted geos (IR, RU, TR, UZ, KZ) score high on both reach
and willingness to pay. They're on Telegram in volume, already spend on crypto,
and their pain is acute: geo-blocked exchanges, KYC flags from shifting IPs.
Risk: proxy-channel audiences use free proxies and may not pay. Counter: attack
the free tier directly in copy. **Rule: lead with the pain, not the product.**

## Why the gates
Every arbitrage decays. The gates are the decay detector. If opt-in cost stays
above €7, the funnel is broken, not the market. If opt-ins don't convert, the
angle is broken, not the product. **Rule: kill early, and kill the right thing.**

## Why cost per opt-in is the metric
Sales take weeks and depend on things we can't control. Opt-ins are immediate and
measure exactly what we're testing: does the page + channel combination work.
Optimising for sales in week one means optimising on noise.
**Rule: one metric until it's under €3.**

## What we don't know
- Affiliate terms change; verify them, don't trust the brief.
- CPM figures are third-party and stale.
- The niche ranking is inference, not proven.
- BHW evidence is mostly unfinished journeys.
**Rule: when unsure, verify against the live API before acting.**
