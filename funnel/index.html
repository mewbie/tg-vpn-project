A VPN guide for crypto users in restricted regions

If you use crypto services, understand the rules and security trade-offs before connecting through a VPN. A VPN is a network tool, not a way around an exchange's eligibility requirements or local law.

What a VPN can and cannot do

  • A VPN routes your connection through a VPN server and can encrypt traffic between your device and that server.
  • It does not change your identity, remove KYC requirements, or guarantee access to a website or exchange.
  • A service may restrict VPN use in its terms. Check those terms and the laws that apply where you are before using one.

A few checks before choosing a provider

  • Read the provider's privacy policy and understand what connection data it retains.
  • Look for clear ownership, supported security protocols, and independently verifiable claims.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication on your accounts.
  • Never share a wallet seed phrase, password, or recovery code with a VPN provider.
