# Admin DM script

Direct placements pay 5–20x more per 1,000 views than the official Telegram Ads
cabinet. That premium exists because it requires a conversation — which is also
why a bot can't replace you here.

## Before you send
Have ready: your bridge page URL, one example creative, and a per-channel budget
number. Never DM without a price in mind — you'll accept whatever they say.

Check first: last post date, average views (not subs), and whether they already
run ads. A channel with 3 ads a day is ad-blind; skip it.

## Opening DM

> Hi — I'm [name]. I run [your thing / a small channel]. I'm booking placements
> for a VPN product this week and your channel is a good fit because [specific
> reason — audience is in [geo] / your crypto posts do well].
>
> I'd like a 24h post, [text + image], one link. Would [date] work, and what's
> your rate?

Three things this does: it's specific (not a mass DM), it names the format so
there's nothing to clarify, and it asks their rate first.

## If no reply after 3 days

> Hi — following up on the VPN placement. Still booking for this week if you
> have a slot. If you'd rather not, no worries — just let me know either way.

"Either way" gets a reply. Silence is the enemy, not a no.

## If they say "make me an offer"

Anchor with CPM maths, not a random number:

> Your last posts are doing about [N] views. At €[X] CPM that's €[Y] for a post —
> is that workable?

Using their own numbers as justification is far more persuasive than "I'll pay €15".

## If they quote double

Don't counter immediately. Ask:

> What's that based on? Just so I understand your pricing.

Then pick one of three moves:

- **Volume:** "If I take three posts this month instead of one, can you do
  €[lower] each?" — this works more often than any other move.
- **Batch proof:** "I'm booking 10 channels this week, so I'm working to a fixed
  per-post budget. €[X] is where I can go."
- **Walk away politely:** "That's above my budget for this test — if you have
  space at €[X] later, I'd still like to try."

The walk-away is real. There are thousands of channels; you don't need this one.

## The close

> Great — confirming: 24h post on [date], [format], link to [your page], €[price],
> paid upfront in USDT/TON. I'll send the creative the night before. Sound right?

Then pay. Get a screenshot of the post once live.

## After the post — this is the part people skip

Thank them, and tell them the result honestly. If it worked, you'll get a better
rate next time and they'll remember you. If it didn't, say so — that's how you
build the relationship that makes booking 10 channels easy by month three.

## Non-negotiables
- Unique sub-ID on every link, always. Without it the spend is unmeasurable.
- Never pay before agreeing the date and format in writing.
- Never buy a second post from a channel that produced zero opt-ins, unless the
  price halves.
