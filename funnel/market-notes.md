# Market notes — background that informs decisions

## The six hard filters a product must pass
1. Free or near-free to start — no deposit gate
2. Pays recurring or revshare, not one-off CPA
3. Instant affiliate approval, no follower minimum
4. Converts on cold mobile traffic in one session
5. Accepts cheap-geo traffic without penalty
6. You can explain it in your own voice

VPN passes all six. Casino fails #1, prop firms fail #2, Binance fails #3.

## Earnings ceiling (calibration, not a promise)
- bottom 50%: €0–100/mo · median: €100–500 · top 25%: €500–2,000
- top 10%: €2,000–8,000 · top 1%: €8,000–40,000 (3–10 channels + team + product)
- **solo affiliate caps at €10–15k/mo** — beyond needs a team or a product
- €20k/mo ≈ five 100k channels at €8 CPM and 20 posts/month

Ceiling multipliers in leverage order: portfolio → own product → team →
revshare base → brand.

## Expansion ladder (after VPN proves out)
VPN → residential proxies → dedicated IP → anti-detect → DNS filtering →
own service. Ticket rises as audience narrows. Never change audience AND
product at once.

## Future acquisition channel: Telegram search squatting
Channels whose username exactly matches a search keyword rank #1 for it. Bots
allow a "bot" suffix without losing rank, and 20 bots per account vs 10 channels.
Documented to have peaked at $22k/month. Half-life 12–24 months because the
keyword space is invisible — the slowest-decaying asymmetry available.

## The structure model (why any of this works)
Four layers: price asymmetry → intermediary position → perishable-to-durable
conversion → decay clock. Decay rate tracks visibility. The method is the entry
fee; the position is the business.
